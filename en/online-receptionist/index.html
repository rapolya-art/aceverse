FAQ

How is an AI Receptionist different from a regular auto-attendant?

An auto-attendant simply plays a recorded message. An AI Receptionist holds a full conversation: understands questions, answers them, books clients for specific times, checks available slots, and sends confirmations.

Is an AI Receptionist suitable for a beauty salon?

Yes, beauty salons are one of the primary use cases. The AI Receptionist books clients with specific stylists, sends day-before reminders, handles cancellations and rescheduling, and integrates with popular salon CRMs.

How many calls can an AI Receptionist handle simultaneously?

An AI Receptionist can handle an unlimited number of simultaneous calls. Unlike a live administrator, it never puts clients on hold.

Can the AI Receptionist voice be customized?

Yes, you can choose a voice from our library or create a unique voice for your brand. Male and female voices with various intonations are available.
