Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI receptionist schedule patients with a specific doctor?

The AI integrates with your scheduling system and sees each doctor's available slots in real time. The patient names the desired doctor or service, and the agent suggests the nearest available times and books the appointment.

Can the AI reschedule or cancel an appointment?

Yes. A patient can call and request to reschedule or cancel. The AI finds the current booking, offers alternative times for rescheduling, or confirms the cancellation and updates the schedule.

How do SMS reminders work?

After booking, the patient receives an SMS with visit details: date, time, doctor, clinic address. A reminder SMS is sent 2 hours before the appointment. The AI can also call the patient to confirm.

What happens when the AI can't answer a question?

If a question is outside the knowledge base or the patient asks to speak with a person, the AI instantly escalates the call to a live operator or clinic administrator.

Does the patient need to provide their phone number?

No. The AI automatically detects the number from the incoming call and finds the patient in the database. If it's a first-time caller, the AI only asks for their name.
