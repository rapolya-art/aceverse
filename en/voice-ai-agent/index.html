FAQ

What is a Voice AI Agent?

A Voice AI Agent is an AI-powered program that can make and receive phone calls, hold natural conversations with clients, book appointments, confirm orders, and integrate with CRM systems. It works 24/7 without breaks or days off.

How much does a Voice AI Agent cost?

The cost depends on call volume and scenario complexity. A basic solution for small business costs significantly less than maintaining a full-time operator. Contact us for a personalized quote.

What languages does the AI Agent support?

Our Voice AI Agents support 20+ languages including English, Ukrainian, Polish, German, and others with natural intonation and deep context understanding.

How quickly can a Voice AI Agent be deployed?

An MVP voice agent can be launched within 1-2 weeks. This includes process audit, scenario setup, integration with your systems, and testing.

Will the AI Agent replace live operators?

The AI Agent handles routine calls: bookings, confirmations, FAQ. Complex or emotional situations are escalated to a live operator. It's a hybrid model that increases team efficiency.
